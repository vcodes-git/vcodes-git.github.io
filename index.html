2. Projects

2.13D Reconstruction of Structures

Python, COLMAP, SQLite, Open3D, scikit-learn · two-person project Apr 2026 · repo ↗

A pipeline that turns ordinary phone video into a 3D Gaussian Splat model you can take measurements from. My parts were the frame matching, the COLMAP database step, point-cloud cleanup, and Gaussian Splatting [2].

  • Sequential frame-to-frame matching broke the reconstruction into fragments. I replaced it with KD-tree nearest-neighbour search over a temporal window of nearby frames, which keeps matching close to O(N log N) instead of the O(N²) of matching every pair.
  • Wrote the script that loads those verified matches into COLMAP's SQLite database to seed its mapper, and initialized bundle adjustment with the OPENCV camera model so the ultra-wide lens distortion could be solved and removed (the building had been bowing inward).
  • Cleaned the sparse point cloud with statistical outlier removal and DBSCAN. This dropped 2.1% of points, mostly floaters in the sky, and lowered mean reprojection error from 0.426 to 0.421 px.
  • Trained 3D Gaussian Splatting for 30,000 iterations from the cleaned cloud. Starting from clean geometry is what kept the splats from filling empty space with floating artifacts.
